1. Quote: “Zed messages received from now on go into one FIFO tagged request or notification” / “Any server-to-client request during replay is answered with `null`.”

Problem: Zed responses are undefined during recovery. A response to a request from the crashed Godot connection could be forwarded to the replacement connection, collide with a new request ID, or be handled differently by implementers.

Exact fix text: “Track server-to-client request IDs by Godot connection. On connection loss, mark outstanding IDs stale. The recovery FIFO preserves requests, notifications, and responses in arrival order. Consume and drop responses to stale IDs; forward a response only when its request belongs to the current connection. Requests received from Godot during replay are answered with `null` and are not forwarded to Zed.”

Problem: A removed file cannot be canonicalized, and unlike Zed URIs, watcher paths have no recorded path-to-key mapping. Implementers may ignore removals, derive keys lexically, or canonicalize an ancestor.

Exact fix text: “Whenever a scan or watcher create successfully canonicalizes a file, record its normalized absolute watcher path to canonical document key. A watcher removal uses that recorded key without canonicalizing the missing file, then deletes the mapping when the document is forgotten. Treat a rename as removal followed by creation.”

Problem: Checking the DAP lock without retaining it creates a race: a DAP session can acquire the lock after the check but before headless Godot is killed.
